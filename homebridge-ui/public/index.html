Bosch account
Connected A Bosch login is saved for this plugin.

Sign in once with your Bosch SingleKey ID. Homebridge then keeps itself logged in automatically.

  1. — opens the Bosch login in a new tab.
  2. After you sign in, the browser tries to open a com.bosch.rrc:// address and shows an error — that is expected. Copy that full address from the address bar (or the error page).
  3. Paste it below and press Connect.
